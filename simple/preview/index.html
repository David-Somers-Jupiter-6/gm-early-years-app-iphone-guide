Help signing in on your iPhone

Having trouble signing in?You are not on your own. We will help you, one step at a time.

Accessibility Start

Getting ready

Before you start

You need these 3 things:

  • The account is for youIt is for the child’s mother or primary carer.
  • Your own NHS loginNot got one? Set up NHS login
  • Your Activation LinkIt comes by text message.

Tip: read this guide on another screen, like a computer. Then sign in on your iPhone.

Step 1 of 8 · GM Early Years App

Step 1 of 8: Open your Activation Link

  1. Open Messages and find the text about your GM Early Years App account.
  2. Tap the link in the text. The App sign-in screen opens.

Stuck here?No text with a link?

Get help
BackHelpNext

Step 2 of 8 · GM Early Years App

Step 2 of 8: Tap Continue to NHS login

  1. Under Parents and carers sign in below, tap Continue to NHS login.
  2. Do not use the buttons further down. They are for staff.

Stuck here?Sign-in screen not opening?

Get help
BackHelpNext

Step 3 of 8 · NHS login

Step 3 of 8: Sign in to NHS login

  1. Asked about cookies? Tap either choice.
  2. Type your NHS login email address and password.
  3. Tap Continue.

Stuck here?Cannot sign in?

Get help
BackHelpNext

Step 4 of 8 · NHS login

Step 4 of 8: Enter your security code

  1. Wait for a text from NHS login with a 6‑digit code.
  2. Tap the code above your keyboard.
  3. Tap Continue.

Stuck here?Code not arrived?

Get help
BackHelpNext

Step 5 of 8 · NHS login

Step 5 of 8: Trust this device

  1. Your own iPhone? Choose Yes, trust this device. If not, choose Not now.
  2. Tap Continue.
  3. Wait while NHS login checks your details.

Stuck here?Error about your details?

Get help
BackHelpNext

Step 6 of 8 · GM Early Years App

Step 6 of 8: Get your App code

  1. Check the last digits shown are your mobile number.
  2. Tap Send Code.
  3. Wait for a text from msverify.

Stuck here?Not your number?

Get help
BackHelpNext

Step 7 of 8 · GM Early Years App

Step 7 of 8: Enter your App code

  1. Tap the code box.
  2. Tap the code above your keyboard. This is a new code, not the NHS login one.
  3. Tap Verify Code.

Stuck here?App not opening?

Get help
BackHelpNext

Step 8 of 8 · All done

Step 8 of 8: You are signed in

  1. If you see Assessment Forms Status, tap Close.
  2. You will see your details. That is everything.

Well done!You can close this guide now.
